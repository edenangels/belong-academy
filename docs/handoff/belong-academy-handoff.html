Belong Academy

The learning academy for the Craveiral and Belong team: short filmed lessons from Pedro, a path built for each department, quick questions that record training hours, and reels. This page is the guide for the showcase and for running the academy afterwards.

Where it lives

Academybelong-academy.pages.devthe live site the team uses
Previewpreview.belong-academy.pages.devsame build, for trying changes first

Works on any phone or computer browser, in English and European Portuguese. Team members sign in with their own account so their progress and hours follow them; "Continue as guest" is for a quick look.

Showcase, about 20 minutes

  1. 0:00 · 2 min

    Sign in and choose a department

    Every person picks their department. Each department has three goals, always in the same order: the team, the land and the project, the guests. The goal builds their path.

  2. 2:00 · 3 min

    Home and the path

    The path shows the modules in order; the next one opens only when the one before is finished. The flame at the top is the learning streak.

  3. 5:00 · 4 min

    A module and a lesson

    Lessons are short videos. The arrow on each lesson opens a summary and chapters that jump to that moment. At the end, two quick questions record the training hours.

  4. 9:00 · 3 min

    A mistake, then earning it

    Answer one question wrong on purpose. The answer is not given away: review the moment in the video, press Continue, and answer again. Missed questions also wait in My learning, under Review.

  5. 12:00 · 2 min

    Module complete

    Finishing a module shows a window with the next module in the person's path.

  6. 14:00 · 2 min

    Reels

    Ten short vertical reels on Belong's values. Swipe or use the arrows; save the ones you like to My learning.

  7. 16:00 · 3 min

    For leaders: the Team view

    Who is doing what, progress per course, scores, hours toward the 40 a year, and who needs attention. Admins can also set an optional field mission on any course.

  8. 19:00 · 1 min

    Certificate and the hours record

    A course certificate is issued when every lesson is done and the score reaches 70%. My learning, under Record, keeps the yearly training hours.

The law

Hours are credited when a lesson's questions are answered and count toward the 40 hours of continuous training a year (Labour Code, art. 131.º). Records name Craveiral as the employer delivering internal training.

Running it after today

Adding a lesson or reel

Upload the video to the Belong folder on Vimeo as unlisted and embeddable, then send us the link. We add it with its summary, chapters and questions in both languages.

Next videos to film

The goals plan lists three or four video ideas for every goal. The thin spots today are maintenance, laundry, kitchen procedures and safety.

Adding people

Team members create their account on the sign-in page. Admins are set in Settings, under Company, by email.

Feedback

Every round of feedback so far is recorded with what changed. Keep sending notes the same way.
